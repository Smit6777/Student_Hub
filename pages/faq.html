📌 General Questions

StudentHub is a digital campus portal created for students of CHARUSAT University. It provides a centralized platform to access academic information, register for events, manage profiles, and stay updated with campus activities.

StudentHub is designed for all students and administrators of CHARUSAT University. Students can register for an account, while administrators have special access to manage students and events.

Yes! StudentHub is completely free for all students. There are no charges for registration, event participation, or using any of the portal features.

👤 Account & Registration

Go to the Register page, fill in your personal details including name, email, department, semester, and enrollment number, create a password, and click "Register Now". You can then log in with your credentials.

Please contact the administrator through the Contact page or email us at smitmakwana6777@gmail.com. We will help you reset your password as soon as possible.

Yes! After logging in, go to the Profile page where you can view and update your personal information, contact details, and bio using the "Update Profile" form.

🎉 Events

Visit the Events page to browse all available events. Each event has a "Register Now" button if registration is open. Click it to register for the event.

Currently, event cancellations need to be done through the administrator. Please reach out via the Contact page or email us with your enrollment number and the event name you wish to cancel.

Yes! Certificates are provided for workshops and hackathons. After the event, certificates will be available through your student dashboard.

🔧 Technical

StudentHub works on all modern browsers including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari. We recommend using the latest version of your browser.

Yes! StudentHub is fully responsive and works on mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.

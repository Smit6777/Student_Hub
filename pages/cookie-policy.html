Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how StudentHub uses cookies and similar storage technologies when you access our campus portal. We believe in complete transparency and do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device by a web server when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your browser, remember your session authentication, and save your visual preferences.

2. Cookies Used by StudentHub

StudentHub uses only strictly necessary and functional cookies. We do not load third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or cross-site telemetry cookies.

Cookie / Storage Key Type Duration Purpose
PHPSESSID Strictly Necessary Session (cleared on browser close or 15 mins inactivity) Maintains your authenticated session securely across pages.
remember_token Functional (Optional) 30 Days Enables seamless login without retyping password if "Remember Me" was checked.
localStorage: theme Preference Persistent Remembers your selection between Light Mode and Dark Mode to avoid screen flash.
localStorage: bannerDismissed Preference Persistent Remembers if you dismissed the top campus announcement banner.
localStorage: cookieConsent Consent Persistent Remembers your acknowledgement of the cookie consent banner.

3. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can manage, inspect, or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

Note: If you disable session cookies, you will not be able to log in to protected areas like your Student Dashboard or Admin Management panels.

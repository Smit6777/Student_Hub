Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Welcome to StudentHub. We respect the privacy of every student, faculty member, and visitor. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and what rights you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization and collect only the information necessary to provide academic and campus event services:

  • Account Information: Full Name, Email Address, and a cryptographically salted password hash when creating a user account.
  • Student Academic Information: Course enrolled, year of study, mobile number, and gender (provided voluntarily during student directory registration).
  • Event Data: Event participation records, workshop sign-ups, and optional event poster uploads.
  • Technical & Session Data: Authentication session cookies (PHPSESSID), voluntary remember-me authentication tokens, and audit logs of administrative actions.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Your information is used strictly for internal campus purposes:

  • Authenticating student and administrator access.
  • Displaying student directories and academic profiles.
  • Coordinating workshop, seminar, and hackathon registrations.
  • Maintaining institutional system audit logs for security and compliance.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or external marketing organizations.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Passwords are encrypted using modern Bcrypt (PASSWORD_DEFAULT) one-way hashing algorithms. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Database queries utilize parameterized prepared statements to defend against SQL Injection vulnerabilities.
  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) tokens protect sensitive forms and administrative mutations.
  • Sensitive session cookies are protected with HttpOnly and SameSite flags.
  • Standard defensive HTTP security headers are transmitted to guard against clickjacking and MIME-sniffing.

4. Cookie Usage

StudentHub uses only strictly essential session cookies and client-side preference storage:

  • PHPSESSID: Keeps you logged in as you navigate between pages.
  • remember_token: Optional cookie set only when you check "Remember Me for 30 days".
  • localStorage: Used strictly to preserve your Light/Dark theme preference and notice dismissal state.

For more details, please review our complete Cookie Policy.

5. Age Requirement

StudentHub is designed exclusively for college and university students. By registering on StudentHub, you verify that you are at least 16 years of age and enrolled in university-level coursework.

6. Your Rights and Account Deletion

Under international privacy principles and data protection standards, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: View all personal data held in your student profile and dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit or update outdated personal details at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You have the right to permanently delete your account and remove your credentials from our database. Logged-in users can execute account deletion anytime via the Delete Account page.
